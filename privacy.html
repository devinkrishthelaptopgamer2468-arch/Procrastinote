Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Procrastinote is a free school planner made by Devin Krish, a student. This page explains what information Procrastinote keeps, why, and what you can do about it.

What we keep

  • Your account: your name, email address, class/grade and password (passwords are stored scrambled, so nobody can read them). If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google.
  • Your planner: timetable, day orders, uniforms, homework, home study schedule, notes and settings, so the same account works on every device.
  • Things you send to friends or classes: chat messages, notes you share, and photos or documents you send.
  • Kept only on your device: your Groq AI key (encrypted in your browser) and pictures you upload into notes.

How it's used

Only to run Procrastinote: logging you in, showing your planner, and delivering messages, notes and files to the people you send them to. We don't sell your information, show ads, or use it to track you.

Who can see it

  • Your planner and notes: only you.
  • Messages, shared notes and files: only you and the people you sent them to.
  • Class chats and files: the members of that class.
  • The site owner can see reported messages, to keep Procrastinote safe.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores accounts, planner data, messages and files.
  • Vercel hosts the website.
  • Google lets you sign in with your Google account, if you choose to.
  • Groq answers AI chat questions, only if you add your own Groq key. What you ask the AI is sent to Groq to get an answer.

Your choices

  • You can change or delete your notes, homework and planner at any time.
  • You can remove your Groq key in Settings → Security.
  • To delete your whole account, contact the site owner and it will be removed.

Children

Procrastinote is made for school students. If you are under 13, please ask a parent or guardian before creating an account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
